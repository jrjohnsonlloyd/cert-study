# Certification Roadmap

Ordered by what to do next. Prices and dates were researched in October 2026 and should be confirmed on the vendor's site before you buy anything.

## Funding, in order of preference

1. **VR&E (Chapter 31).** Ask your counselor to add exam vouchers and training to your rehabilitation plan, tied to your job goal of technical writer or GRC documentation analyst.
2. **CompTIA Academic Store** with your ASU `.edu` email. Up to about 50% off CompTIA vouchers.
3. Free vendor training wherever it exists (Microsoft Learn, Vanta and Drata partner training, Fortinet Veterans Program).

## Path

| # | Certification | Code | Status | Approx. exam cost | Why it is on the path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CompTIA Network+ | N10-009 | Studying | $399 (less with academic pricing) | Networking foundation; backs the homelab and network SOPs |
| 2 | CompTIA Security+ | SY0-701 | Studying | $439 (less with academic pricing) | Baseline security cert; the most-requested credential for security work |
| 3 | Vanta and Drata partner training | n/a | Not started | Free | Most direct credential for SOC 2 documentation work |
| 4 | ISC2 CGRC | CGRC | Not started | $599 | Formalizes NIST 800-53 and RMF control mapping. Can be taken as an Associate of ISC2 until the experience requirement is met |
| 5 | ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Implementer | PECB or similar | Not started | Varies by provider | ISO 27001 is better known than SOC 2 outside the US |
| 6 | Microsoft SC-300 (optional) | SC-300 | Not started | $165 | Entra ID and Conditional Access; serves both admin work and GRC evidence |
| 7 | CompTIA CySA+ (optional, later) | CS0-004 | Not started | $439 | Analyst-level, taken after Net+ and Sec+. Passing it renews both, so take it within 3 years of passing Security+ |
| 8 | Cisco CCNA (optional) | 200-301 | Not started | $300 | Deeper networking, if hands-on network work becomes a focus |

## Timing notes

- **Security+:** you plan to take SY0-701 before it retires (expected around June 2027), so no SY0-801 question bank is needed. SY0-801 is expected around November 17, 2026.
- **CySA+:** CS0-004 is current. Passing it renews Security+ and Network+.
- **CMMC:** not on the path. The work generally requires being in the US, and the program is being reorganized.

## Alongside the certs

- ASU Technical Communication minor (BA English, expected 2028). Get it approved in your VR&E plan.
- Google Technical Writing One and Two (free online courses).
- Write the Docs community (Slack, job board).
